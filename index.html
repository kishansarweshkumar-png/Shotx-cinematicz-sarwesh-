Founder’s Story

The Journey Behind Shotx Cinematicz (English)

The year was 2021. I had a deep interest in dance, but along with dance, something else started attracting my attention. Whenever I watched a movie or a video, my focus was not only on the story, but on the visuals, camera work, creativity and editing behind it. At that time, I did not realize that this small curiosity would become my biggest passion. After one year, my cousin's wedding happened. After the wedding was over, I thought of creating a memorable video from those moments. But at that time, I didn't even know how to properly combine two videos. I started learning through YouTube and slowly learned video editing, cinematography, storytelling and visual creation. In 2025, I realized that there was a need for a professional video production company which could provide high-quality cinematic services. Then I decided to create Shotx Cinematicz. The decision was not easy. Many people laughed at my dream and told me to focus only on studies. But I believed in myself. I started everything on my own, faced challenges and continued moving forward. Today, Shotx Cinematicz is a result of passion, hard work and dedication.
